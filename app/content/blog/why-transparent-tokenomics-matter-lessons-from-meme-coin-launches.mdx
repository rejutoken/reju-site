# Why Transparent Tokenomics Matter, Lessons from Meme Coin Launches

The crypto space offers exciting opportunities for innovation and growth. Recent activity with tokens like Stoic on Solana shows common patterns in high risk speculative launches.

## A Recent Example from the Meme Coin Space

Here is a chart from Stoic during its trading session.

![Stoic token chart showing sharp price drop](/stoic-dump-1.png)

The creator account sold a significant amount of tokens into the market. This action reduced the price quickly.

![Stoic creator wallet activity showing large sell](/stoic-dump-2.png)

Many new investors hoped to add value back into the token after the move. This pattern appears frequently in the meme coin sector. Creators often sell large holdings after initial hype builds the price.

## The Common Behavior in Speculative Launches

This sell off pattern occurs regularly on platforms like pump.fun. A large percentage of tokens experience rapid pumps followed by sharp drops when early holders exit. Investors face high risk in these situations because of limited visibility into token holdings and intentions.
