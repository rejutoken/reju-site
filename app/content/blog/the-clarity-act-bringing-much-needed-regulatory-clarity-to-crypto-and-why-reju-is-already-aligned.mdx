# The CLARITY Act: Bringing Much-Needed Regulatory Clarity to Crypto and Why REJU Is Already Aligned

The cryptocurrency industry has long operated in a regulatory gray area that stifles innovation, creates uncertainty for developers, and leaves investors exposed. Enter the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act (better known as the CLARITY Act), a landmark piece of bipartisan legislation designed to finally provide clear rules of the road for digital assets in the United States.


## What Is the CLARITY Act?

The CLARITY Act aims to establish a comprehensive market structure framework for digital assets. It clarifies the division of oversight between the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC).

Tokens that function as investment contracts fall under SEC jurisdiction.
Digital commodities (mature network tokens) are placed under CFTC oversight.

This functional approach recognizes the unique nature of blockchain assets while bringing them in line with traditional financial market protections.


## Current Status and Path Forward

TThe House of Representatives passed its version of the bill in 2025. In the Senate, the Banking Committee advanced a version in May 2026 on a bipartisan 15-9 vote. The bill is now on the Senate Legislative Calendar, with ongoing negotiations to merge texts and address remaining issues (including ethics provisions and stablecoin rules).

If passed, it would move quickly toward presidential signature. However, the legislative window is tight, the Senate faces an August recess, making the coming weeks critical for 2026 passage. You can show support and stay updated via official channels and advocacy pages like clarityact.net.


### What Will the CLARITY Act Affect?

The bill would reshape how digital assets are issued, traded, and regulated:

*	Issuance and offerings: Clearer rules for initial sales and when tokens transition from securities to commodities.
*	Exchanges and intermediaries: Registration requirements and customer protections for platforms handling digital commodities.
*	Stablecoins and payment assets: Dedicated frameworks for permitted payment stablecoins.
*	DeFi and innovation: Safe harbors for non-custodial developers and protocols, reducing the risk of overreach.
*	Secondary markets: Regulatory certainty for trading mature tokens.

Overall, it aims to keep innovation on U.S. soil rather than pushing it overseas.


### How Does It Protect Consumers and the Market?

The CLARITY Act strengthens protections by:

*	Requiring robust customer safeguards on registered entities (similar to traditional finance).
*	Providing legal clarity that reduces scams and enforcement uncertainty.
*	Establishing accountability while preserving the decentralized ethos of crypto.
*	Protecting investors through transparent rules around disclosures, custody, and market integrity.

This creates a safer environment for everyday Americans to participate in the digital economy.


### What New Token Developers Should Consider

If you're building or launching a token project in 2026 and beyond, the CLARITY Act (once passed) will make compliance more predictable. Key considerations include:

*	Design for functionality, Focus on utility and network maturity to qualify for digital commodity treatment over time.
*	Transparent disclosures, Provide clear information on token mechanics, allocations, vesting, and lifecycle.
*	Compliance from day one, Build with registration pathways, AML/KYC readiness, and investor protections in mind.
*	Avoid gray areas, Structure offerings to minimize securities classification risks where appropriate.
*	Plan for dual jurisdiction, Understand SEC vs. CFTC rules depending on the stage of your token.

Early alignment with these principles will reduce legal risk and build long-term trust.
